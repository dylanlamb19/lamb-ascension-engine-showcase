Full-stack project by Dylan Lamb

Lead acquisition & outreach automation for small service businesses

Finds local prospects through Google Places, scores how well each one fits, and runs outreach sequences that require the owner's approval before any first contact. A background worker handles follow-ups and reply sync; a Sentinel checker guards do-not-contact rules, bounces, and worker health.

Production source is private — code walkthrough available on request.

Features

Prospect discovery

Searches local businesses by vertical and area via the Google Places API.

Fit scoring

Rates each prospect from research data. Never invents revenue, ad spend, or lead counts.

Owner approval

No first contact goes out until the business owner approves the sequence.

Background worker

Sends scheduled follow-ups and syncs replies into each prospect's timeline.

Sentinel

Read-only invariant checks for do-not-contact, bounces, and worker health — reports, never silently auto-fixes.

CI checks

GitHub Actions runs Sentinel in CI, on a schedule, and after each deploy.
